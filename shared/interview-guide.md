# Question Bank: Getting Know-How Out of Experienced Heads

Use together with `decision-logic-capture.md` (probing techniques and pattern structure). These questions are conversation openers, not a form.

Experienced GMs often say "it depends" or "you just know". The job of the interview is to turn that into situations, signals, actions, reasons and numbers. Use the techniques below, one question at a time, in the GM's language.

## Session rules

- Conversations can be 5 minutes or an hour. Follow the GM's energy; it is fine to jump between domains — classify afterwards.
- Ask one question per message. Wait for the answer. Follow up at least twice before moving on.
- The GM may answer by voice dictation, in any language or a mix of languages, in rough sentences. Do not correct their language; structure it afterwards.
- Reflect back in 2–3 lines what you heard before writing a card: "So the rule is… because… except when… Correct?"
- End each conversation with "What I learned", gaps noticed, and one suggested next topic.

## Techniques

1. **Critical decision**: "Tell me about a time when… What did you notice first? What options did you have? Why did you pick that one? What happened?" → case card + rules.
2. **Novice vs expert**: "What does a new store manager usually do here? What do you do differently?" → rule with a "Watch out".
3. **First 10 minutes**: "When you walk into a store, what do you look at in the first 10 minutes, in order?" → playbook.
4. **Signals and thresholds**: "At what number do you start to worry? At what number do you act?" → benchmark.
5. **Red lines**: "What must a manager never do, even under pressure?" → red-line card.
6. **Teach-back**: summarise the rule and ask the GM to correct it. Their corrections are the most valuable part.
7. **Last note**: "If you left tomorrow, what three things must your successor know about this brand?"
8. **Document mining**: read a deck, SOP or P&L review in `sources/`, then ask "Which parts of this really matter? What is missing from the document that you know?"

## Question bank by domain

Pick 3–5 per session. Adapt to the brand.

### D1 Strategy & brand direction
- Who is our guest, and why do they choose us over the nearest competitor?
- Which idea did you say no to, even though it looked good? Why?
- How do you turn the group's plan into three priorities for the brand?

### D2 P&L & commercial
- When a store's profit drops, which lines do you check first, in what order?
- What are the normal ranges for food cost, labour and rent in our stores? When do you act?
- Tell me about a price increase or a discount that went well, and one that went badly.
- How do you think about delivery margin after commission?

### D3 Operations excellence
- What do you look at in the first 10 minutes of a store visit?
- How do you prepare a store for a peak day or a long weekend?
- Which standard slips first when a store is under pressure? How do you catch it early?

### D4 Guest experience & channels
- What do you do when a store's rating drops on a delivery platform?
- What is our rule for compensating a guest after a mistake?
- How do you grow our own channel and loyalty members, not only delivery platforms?

### D5 Marketing, menu & innovation
- How do you decide a new menu item is a success? After how many weeks?
- Tell me about a campaign that increased sales but lost money.
- When do you remove an item from the menu?

### D6 People & leadership
- How do you spot a store manager who can become an area manager?
- Tell me about a hard people decision you made. What did you learn?
- What keeps good crew from leaving in our brand?

### D7 Growth & network
- What makes a good site for our brand? What made a bad one fail?
- How long should a new store take to break even? When do you close one?

### D8 Supply chain, cost & food safety
- What do you do when a key supplier fails on a busy day?
- Where does waste hide in our stores?
- Which food-safety rules are never negotiable?

### D9 Partners & stakeholders
- How do you negotiate with a mall landlord? What works in our market?
- How do you get a brand owner or franchisor to agree to a local change?
- How do you present bad news to the executive committee?

### D10 Risk, crisis & compliance
- Tell me about the worst crisis you handled. What did you do in the first hour?
- What is your plan when the POS or the delivery tablets go down at peak?
- How do you handle a viral negative post about a store?

### X1 Decision making
- When you must decide with incomplete data, what do you do?
- Which decisions do you make yourself, and which do you push down to area managers?
