## Problem type
A store's monthly profit is clearly below plan. I treat it as real only when it lasts 3 weeks or more; one bad week is noise.

## First look
1. Sales split: bills (traffic) vs average check — never look at sales alone.
2. Same weeks in nearby stores of ours and the mall's footfall — is it the store or the location?
3. Labour hours by hour vs sales by hour.
4. Food cost and waste lines.
5. Then I visit at peak, unannounced.

## Diagnosis
- Bills down, nearby stores also down → market or mall issue → talk to the landlord, check competitors, do not punish the team.
- Bills down only in this store → service or quality issue → check ratings, speed, staff turnover, the manager.
- Bills flat, average check down → promotion mix or upselling stopped → check discount share.
- Sales fine, profit down → cost issue → labour scheduling or waste first; rent last (rarely moves).

## Options
Fix the roster; coach or replace the manager; local marketing; menu/price action; renegotiate rent; wait one more month.

## What matters most
Guest experience before short-term margin. I do not cut hours at peak to save labour — it kills traffic next month.

## Decision rule
If it is a store problem and the manager is new, coach first for 4 weeks. If the manager is experienced and two causes are on the store side, change the manager. If it is a location problem for 6 months or more, start the relocation/closure case.

## How I handle it
Agree 2–3 actions with the area manager, written, with dates. Review weekly by phone, monthly in person.

## Check & adjust
Bills trend after 4 weeks. If no change, the diagnosis was wrong — go back to First look.

## Boundaries
Not for the first 6 months after opening (use the new-store ramp-up pattern). Long holidays distort comparisons — compare with the same holiday last year.
