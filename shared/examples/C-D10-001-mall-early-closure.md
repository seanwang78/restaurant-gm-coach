## Situation
On a Saturday at 14:00, a mall told tenants it would close at 16:00 for safety reasons. Three of our stores were inside; evening is usually 40% of their sales. Staff were worried about getting home.

## Options considered
1. Close at 16:00 and send everyone home.
2. Close dine-in, keep a small team for delivery from a back entrance.
3. Move delivery zones to the nearest street store and send that store extra staff who live nearby.

## Decision
Option 3. Staff safety first: mall teams went home by 16:30. The street store 2 km away took the delivery zones; two volunteers who lived nearby moved there with paid transport.

## Result
About 60% of the lost evening sales were recovered through delivery (approximate). No staff stayed in the closed area.

## Lesson
Decide people safety in the first 15 minutes, then sales. Keep a list of which street store can take each mall store's delivery zone before you need it.
