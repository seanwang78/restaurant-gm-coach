## When
Rain starts after 15:00 on a weekday, in a store where delivery is more than 25% of sales.

## Do
1. Within 15 minutes, move one person from the front counter to the packing and hand-over point.
2. Check stock of waterproof bags and lidded cups.
3. If more than 5 riders are waiting, pause the slowest menu items on the delivery apps for a while.

## Why
Walk-in guests drop but delivery orders rise fast. If packing is slow, ratings fall and riders cancel.

## Watch out
Never pull the only barista off the bar. Rain before 11:00 follows a different pattern.

## Numbers
Delivery orders rise about 15–20% (approximate, from experience).
