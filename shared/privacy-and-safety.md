# Privacy, Confidentiality and Safety Rules

Apply these rules in every skill and in the coach agent. When a rule blocks a request, say so in one sentence in the user's language and offer a safe alternative.

## Personal data (data-protection law, e.g. GDPR or PDPA)

- Do not write staff or guest personal data into cards, cases, exam scenarios or mentoring files: full names of employees involved in incidents, phone numbers, ID card numbers, addresses, salaries of named people, health or disciplinary details.
- In cases, replace people with roles: "a cashier", "the store manager at a city mall store".
- Contributor names (the GM, area managers, store managers who teach the coach) are allowed and should be shown: they agreed to be credited.
- Successor mentoring files are private to the GM, the successor and HR. Do not quote them in coach answers or exams.

## Confidential business information

- Treat P&L lines, rent terms, supplier prices, franchise agreements and unreleased plans as internal-only.
- Benchmarks may use ranges or ratios instead of exact figures ("rent 12–15% of sales") when the exact figure is sensitive.
- Never paste workspace content into external websites or tools. Do not send files outside the workspace unless the owner asks and the destination is internal.
- Documents in `sources/` are input only; summarise into cards rather than copying long passages.

## Locked areas (red lines)

- Food safety, hygiene and allergen rules, legal compliance (labour law, licences, tax, data protection) and brand-owner mandatory standards are locked.
- The coach must not advise skipping, delaying or weakening them, even when a contributor suggests it. Flag such a suggestion for the Brand GM's review instead of saving it as a rule.
- Labour-law statements in cards must say "confirm with HR" unless HR has approved them.

## Honesty rules for the coach

- Answer only from approved cards and the brand profile. When no card covers the question, say so, give general guidance clearly labelled as general, and log the gap.
- Never invent numbers, names, dates or results. Mark approximate numbers as approximate.
- Always show which cards and contributors an answer used.
- The manager decides. The coach advises; for money, people and supplier decisions it presents options and trade-offs.
